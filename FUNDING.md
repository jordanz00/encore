# Funding

Encore is an open-source public-good project. Sustainability requires deliberate, mission-aligned funding. This document describes how we fund the project and how donors are recognized.

Full rationale: [`docs/rfcs/008-funding-governance.md`](docs/rfcs/008-funding-governance.md).

## Funding sources, in priority order

### 1. Grants

Targeted grant funding from organizations whose mission aligns with ours:

- [NLnet NGI Zero](https://nlnet.nl/) — Core, Commons, Entrust
- [Open Technology Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/)
- [Sovereign Tech Fund](https://www.sovereigntechfund.de/) — including the Resilience program
- [Mozilla Open Source Support (MOSS)](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/moss/)
- [FUTO](https://futo.org/)

Grant applications are coordinated by the maintainer council. The first application targets v0.1.

### 2. Recurring donations

- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors) — both project umbrella and individual maintainers
- [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/) — fiscal sponsorship + transparent ledger
- [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/) — recurring donations without platform cut

### 3. Subscription revenue (when Encore-hosted launches)

- Premium / Family / Student / HiFi tiers (see [RFC 006](docs/rfcs/006-payments-payouts.md))
- Platform overhead capped at ≤ 10% of subscription net
- Excess overhead funds the project (editorial, T+S, infra)

### 4. Foundation grants (Phase 3+)

When the project moves into a non-profit foundation, the foundation will accept grants and redistribute for explicit work (editorial salaries, T+S team, infra costs).

### 5. Service donations

We will accept service donations (e.g. CDN credit, payment processor fee waivers) **only if**:

- They do not influence product decisions.
- The donor and terms are publicly disclosed in this file.

We do not accept:

- Corporate sponsorship that requires board representation, approval rights, or non-public terms.
- Donations from companies whose business model conflicts with our mission (surveillance ad tech, exclusive label deals that disadvantage indies).

## Where the money goes

Published quarterly:

- Editorial team stipends
- Trust + safety lead
- Infrastructure (CDN, hosting, observability)
- Security audit budget
- Conference + community travel

Until we have funding to publish, we publish $0 in / $0 out.

## Conflict-of-interest policy

- Maintainers must disclose any income from Encore donors.
- Service donors are listed publicly with the donation terms.
- No private side-deals.
