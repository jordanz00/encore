The ask

$250K founding-patron round. Foundation capital, not equity.

We are raising foundation grant capital, not equity. Encore cannot be sold. Angel checks, strategic partners, and grant capital fund the $250K patron round — prototype live today.

"I watched Bandcamp get bought twice. I watched my friends earn $0.003 a stream while Spotify's CEO put a billion dollars into a military AI startup. So I built the thing I wished existed."

Encore is licensed AGPL, owned by a non-profit foundation, and engineered by someone who uploads to it. Every product decision answers one question: does this maximize what the artist deposits in their bank account?

Founding texts · written before first patron dollar

We never use profits or reserves to invest in weapons, war, or military technology. That covenant is in our founding documents — not marketing copy. Musicians left Spotify when its CEO put $1B into military AI; Encore is foundation-owned with no founder equity to weaponize.
