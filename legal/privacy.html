Privacy Policy (beta)

Encore collects the minimum needed to run accounts, playback, and payouts.

What we collect

  • Account email, handle, and session data
  • Playback events for user-centric allocation (when enabled)
  • Payment metadata via Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Waitlist email if you join from the marketing site

What we avoid

No sale of personal data. No ad-tech surveillance stack.
